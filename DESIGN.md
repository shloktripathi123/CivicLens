# CivicTrail Design System

Direction: public-record broadsheet. Reads like a printed record, not a dashboard.

## Tokens (apps/web/app/globals.css)
- Paper `#f6f1e6`, ink `#1a1915`, muted `#55514a` (7.0:1), accent deep green `#1c4a3a` (8.9:1), focus `#8a4b00` (6.0:1), hairline `#c9bfa7`.
- No party colours. One accent only.

## Type
- Headings: Newsreader (serif). Body/UI: Public Sans. Reading measure 68ch, page max width 1080px.

## Components
- Masthead with double rule; nav as plain text links.
- Lists are ruled rows, not cards. Radius 0 everywhere.
- Citations are footnote-style source lines; credibility grade is a stamped square (dashed for C/D) that opens the grading dialog.
- Law vs not-a-law is a chip; enacted laws are filled green.

## Rules
- No gradients, glass, shadows, rounded containers, eyebrow text, or glyph icons; labels carry meaning in words.
- Bill/long-title pages: number as h1, title as lead paragraph.
